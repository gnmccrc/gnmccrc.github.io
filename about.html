About Us

Kenyan not-for-profit organization

The Green Network Foundation is a Kenyan not-for-profit organization whose aim is to improve the livelihoods of local communities by building their capacity to come up with home-grown solutions to local problems.

Our Story

The Green Network Foundation was registered in 2017 as a National NGO in the Office of the President under the Ministry of Interior and Coordination of National Government (NGO Coordination Act).

We are a purpose-driven organization, and it follows that our expression of that purpose is to transform lives. The organization supports and empowers vulnerable and disadvantaged people.

We are a community-led initiative that aims to equip communities and marginalized people with the tools they need to find better health, safety, prosperity and opportunity to lead fulfilling and happy lives.

Read More
OUR MISSION

To engage extensively on a multi-faceted Sustainable Development and Climate Change Action-related activities that would help bridge poverty gap, mitigate Climate Change and empower the youth around the world.

We work towards harnessing unrealized economic potential, unutilized indigenous resources and knowledge. These interventions are undertaken in line with community needs and priorities.

Our Values

What We Stand For

Non-Discrimination

No one will be discriminated against on the basis of his/her HIV/AIDS status, Gender, Religion, Age or Race. Our association and partnership with other people will not be regulated or controlled by these factors.

Community-Led

We are a community-led initiative that aims to equip communities and marginalized people with the tools they need to find better health, safety, prosperity and opportunity.

Empowerment

We support and empower vulnerable and disadvantaged people through building their capacity to come up with home-grown solutions to local problems.

Sustainability

We work towards harnessing unrealized economic potential, unutilized indigenous resources and knowledge in line with community needs and priorities.

Integrity

The organization operates on the basis of its constitution which sets out its objectives, membership criteria, the management structure and the day to day running of the organization.

Environmental Stewardship

Thematic Areas

Our Focus Areas

The organization implements projects under these thematic areas in line with community needs and priorities.

Water, Sanitation and Hygiene

Safe drinking water and proper sanitation prevent waterborne diseases and improve overall wellbeing.

Sustainable Livelihoods

Supporting communities to address threats to food and livelihood security.

Environment and Climatic Change

Community training, drought-resistant trees, protection of water catchments.

Food Security and Nutrition

Integrated support to strengthen local governance and market-responsive livelihoods.

Advocacy on Rights Issues

Supporting causes, influencing decisions and policies related to human rights.
