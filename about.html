About Us

Good News Mission Community Church & Rehabilitation Centres (G.N.M.C.C.)

Established in 1993, Good News Mission Community Church (G.N.M.C.C.) is a Christ-centered Pentecostal ministry dedicated to the spiritual transformation of lives across Kenya. From our humble beginnings in 2001 to our current multi-branch operations, we remain committed to fulfilling the Great Commission through evangelism, discipleship, and compassionate community service.

Where We Began (1993)

The story of Good News Mission Community Church (G.N.M.C.C.) isn't just about buildings; it's about a deep-seated calling that took root back in 1993. When we registered under the Societies Act, we weren't looking to simply start another organization. We were driven by a singular, urgent mission: to take the Gospel to every corner of Kenya, reaching people that society often overlooks.

The Days of Humble Beginnings

If you visited us in 2001, you wouldn't have seen the structures we have today. You would have found a small, dedicated group of believers gathered under a simple iron-sheet roof. It was hot in the sun and loud in the rain, but the spirit in that place was unmistakable. Those early days taught us that a church isn't defined by its walls, but by the lives being changed inside them. By 2005, as our family grew, we knew it was time to build something more permanent,not for our own comfort, but to better serve the community that was quickly outgrowing our small space.

Where We Stand Today

Since those early years, the work has expanded in ways we could have only imagined. We've grown from one small gathering into a network of branches stretching from Nairobi and Eldoret to Isiolo and Migori.

But more importantly, our "pulpit" has moved beyond the church doors. We have spent the last three decades walking into the places where hope is thinnest:

  • In Prisons: We have stood with over 26,000 men and women behind bars, sharing the truth that their past doesn't have to dictate their future.

  • In Schools: We have mentored more than 30,000 students, helping them build a foundation of character and faith.

  • In the Community: Whether it is praying at a hospital bedside or providing water tanks for clean storage, we believe the Gospel must be lived out in practical, hands-on ways.

Leadership & Management Structure

The ministry of Good News Mission Community Church & Rehabilitation Centre is overseen by the Presiding Bishop together with a team of church leaders and ministry coordinators. The leadership provides spiritual guidance, direction, and oversight for the church's programs and outreach ministries.

The church leadership works together to coordinate evangelism, discipleship, prison ministry, school outreach, chaplaincy training, and community support initiatives across the different regions where the church operates.

What We Stand For

Community

We are committed to work with and exist in a community: Where we encourage fellowship; Uphold the family and embrace unity.

Our Objectives

Strategic Ministry Framework

The Good News Mission Community Church & Rehabilitation Centre (G.N.M.C.C.) pursues its vision through a strategic ministry framework focused on discipleship, community transformation and leadership development. These objectives guide our mission to proclaim the Gospel and serve communities across Kenya.

  • Strengthen families and mentor the next generation through children's, youth and family ministries.
  • Promote discipleship and spiritual growth through biblical teaching, small groups and pastoral care.
  • Expand evangelism, missions and church planting to reach more communities with the Gospel of Jesus Christ.
  • Respond to community needs through education initiatives, social outreach and compassionate ministries.
  • Develop strong leadership, transparent governance and sustainable systems that support long-term ministry impact.
  • Equip believers to live out their faith and serve society as agents of transformation through the power of the Holy Spirit.
