Our Initiatives

Current Projects

We facilitate the implementation of integrated livelihood support and resilience programs to help targeted communities become less vulnerable to social, economic, environmental, cultural and health risks.

Food Security Initiative

Turkwel Dam Revitalization

The county is reviving Turkwel Dam for fishing, providing fingerlings and fishing boats to local farmers. The Green Network Foundation aims at providing fingerlings and technical support to fish farmers.

Target

500 households

Progress

320 households reached

Other Community Projects

Tree Nursery Establishment

Establishing tree nurseries to increase the provision of seedlings for reforestation and protection of water catchments.

Target: 10,000 seedlings

Community Climate Training

Carrying out community training on climate change adaptation and mitigation strategies for sustainable livelihoods.

Target: 500 community members

Drought-Resistant Crops

Research and dissemination of drought-resistant crops to help farmers cope with changing climate conditions.

Target: 5 crop varieties

Vaccination Campaigns

Regular vaccination campaigns to protect livestock from diseases and ensure healthy herds for pastoral communities.

Target: 2,000 livestock

River Bank Protection

Protection of water catchments, riverbanks, swamps and fragile lands from degradation and encroachment.

Target: 10km riverbanks

Advocacy Programs

Advocacy on rights issues, raising awareness about human rights and protecting marginalized groups.
