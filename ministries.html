Ministry Area 5

Hospital Ministry

Sharing the love of Christ with the sick, the hurting and their families through prayer, encouragement and compassionate care. We visit hospitals to offer spiritual support, bring hope in difficult moments and remind patients that they are not alone. Through faith and kindness, we minister healing, comfort and strength.
