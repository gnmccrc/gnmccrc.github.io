About Us

Good News Mission Community Church & Rehabilitation Centres (G.N.M.C.C.)

G.N.M.C.C. is an evangelical ministry devoted to the proclamation of the Gospel of our Lord Jesus Christ and the spiritual transformation of lives through faith, discipleship and compassionate services .

WHO WE ARE

Registered in 1993 as a Pentecostal Church in Kenya under the Societies Act of Kenya and issued a certificate of Registration(Societies Act). The focus of the church is to target all people across Kenya and Outside with the word of God.

Geographical Operations:G.N.M.C.C has branch churches in Nairobi, Isiolo, Eldoret, Machakos, Nakuru, Kisii, Narok, Kericho, Mabera, Migori, Kehancha, Ntimaru Kenya.

What We Do

Our Ministry Focus

We carry out initiatives under these key ministry areas to nurture faith, serve our communities and meet spiritual and practical needs.

Evangelism/Outreach & Crusades.

Proclaiming the Gospel of our Lord Jesus Christ transforms lives and brings hope to communities. We conduct evangelistic outreach and preaching events to spread God's Word.

Chaplains Training

Our Chaplaincy Ministry is dedicated to bringing the presence, compassion and peace of Christ into the places where it is needed most. We believe that ministry doesn't just happen on Sundays — it happens in hospitals, prisons, workplaces and during times of crisis.

Men Prison Ministry

Our Prison Ministry is founded on the belief that no one is beyond the reach of God’s grace. Following the biblical mandate to remember those in prison, we go beyond the walls to share the life-changing message of the Gospel with those who are often forgotten by society.

Women Prison Ministry

Our Prison Ministry is a heart-led mission to bring the light of Christ into the darkest corners of correctional facilities. We believe that no wall is too high and no life is too broken for God's grace to reach. We are honored to serve Women inmates, providing hope to all who seek it.

Transforming Lives Through Ministry

Through the grace of God and the dedication of our church family, G.N.M.C.C continues to reach many lives through different ministries including prison outreach, school ministry, evangelism, and children programs. These efforts are helping spread the Gospel, transform lives, and bring hope to communities.

Prison Ministry

We reach out to inmates with the message of hope and restoration through Jesus Christ. Our prison ministry provides spiritual guidance, prayer, counseling, and discipleship to help individuals rebuild their lives and discover a new purpose in Christ.

  • Regular prison visitations
  • Bible teaching and discipleship
  • Prayer and counseling sessions
  • Encouraging rehabilitation and restoration

School Outreach

Our school ministry focuses on guiding young people toward strong moral values and a personal relationship with God. Through school visits, mentorship, and spiritual encouragement, we help students grow in faith and character.

  • School fellowship programs
  • Mentorship and life guidance
  • Sharing the Gospel with students
  • Encouraging positive values and leadership

Evangelism

Evangelism is at the heart of our mission. We actively share the Gospel through community outreach, open-air meetings, and personal evangelism, bringing the message of salvation to people in different communities.

  • Community outreach programs
  • Door-to-door evangelism
  • Prayer and spiritual support
  • Sharing the message of salvation

Children Ministry

We are passionate about nurturing children in the knowledge and love of God. Our children's ministry creates a safe and joyful environment where young hearts learn biblical truths and grow in faith.

  • Bible lessons and storytelling
  • Worship and praise for children
  • Character and spiritual development
  • Fun activities and learning programs

Where We Started

Like many growing churches, G.N.M.C.C started with humble beginnings. In 2001, the congregation gathered in a modest iron-sheet structure for worship. As the church community expanded, plans for a more permanent and spacious sanctuary began and construction of the new church development started in 2005.

Current Construction Phase(Land Acquisition and Future Expansion)

As part of our continued vision for growth and service to the community, the church has successfully acquired land for future development.This milestone marks the beginning of a new phase in the journey of G.N.M.C.C. Plans are underway to develop this space into a center that will serve both the congregation and the wider community. The vision includes the construction of a new and improved church sanctuary, as well as facilities for a hospital and a school that will support spiritual growth, education, and community wellbeing. This development reflects our commitment to building not only a place of worship but also institutions that will positively impact lives for generations to come.
