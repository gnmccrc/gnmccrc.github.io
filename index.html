About Us

Kenyan not-for-profit organization transforming lives

The Green Network Foundation is a Kenyan not-for-profit organization whose aim is to improve the livelihoods of local communities by building their capacity to come up with home-grown solutions to local problems.

WHO WE ARE

Registered in 2017 as a National NGO in the Office of the President under the Ministry of Interior and Coordination of National Government (NGO Coordination Act). We support and empower vulnerable and disadvantaged people through community-led initiatives.

Geographical Operations: Turkana County, West Pokot County, Transzoia County, Kakamega County, Homabay County, Kenya.

Non-Discrimination Policy: No one will be discriminated against on the basis of HIV/AIDS status, Gender, Religion, Age or Race.

What We Do

Our Thematic Areas

We implement projects under these key thematic areas to address community needs and priorities.

Water, Sanitation & Hygiene

Safe drinking water and proper sanitation prevent waterborne diseases and improve overall wellbeing. We invest in water infrastructure and promote good sanitation practices.

Sustainable Livelihoods

We support communities to address threats to food and livelihood security by facilitating integrated livelihood support and resilience programs.

Environment & Climate Change

We carry out community training on climate change, establish drought-resistant trees, protect water catchments, and promote sustainable agriculture.

Food Security & Nutrition

We provide downscaled weather information, farm inputs, drought-resistant crops, and promote agroforestry and climate-smart farming practices.

Climate Risk Profile

Case Study from West Pokot

Climate Change Impacts

  • Unreliable bimodal rainfall patterns
  • Constant failure of maize and sorghum crops in lowlands due to droughts
  • Increased floods and massive soil erosion
  • Cross-border livestock migration causing family unit breakup
  • Reduced water volume in rivers (Kerio, Muruny, Weiwei, Nzoia)

Our Interventions

  • Forestry: Community training, drought-resistant trees, tree nurseries
  • Water: Construction of dams and water pans, protection of water towers
  • Agriculture: Drought-resistant crops, water harvesting, agroforestry
  • Livestock: Breeding climate-adapted animals, vaccination campaigns
  • Infrastructure: Climate-proofed infrastructure, disaster preparedness
